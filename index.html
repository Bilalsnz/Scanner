Signal logic

Deterministic research-priority heuristic. Maximum 100 points, not calibrated as a probability or safety score.

DETERMINISTIC
Volume / liquidity pressure · up to 40 points

At least 0.35× turnover adds 15 points, at least 1× adds 25, and at least 3× adds 40. Only the highest matching tier applies. Calculated only when positive liquidity and volume are reported. High turnover can reflect churn, arbitrage, or manipulation.

Buy / sell count imbalance · 30 points

Requires at least 20 reported swaps and a buy share of at least 67% or at most 33%. Counts do not measure trade sizes, unique traders, or net capital flows.

Absolute price movement · up to 30 points

A 10% move adds 9 points, 20% adds 18, and 50% adds 30, using the highest matching tier. Positive and negative moves are treated alike. These are research triggers, not directional calls.

Liquidity integrity

Zero, missing, and thin liquidity are separate warnings. Missing liquidity is never treated as a confirmed $0 pool, and liquidity does not contribute to the attention index.

Field completeness

This percentage counts the presence of selected provider fields and addresses only. It does not verify correctness, authenticity, market depth, or ZEC backing.
